# 🍅 Fair-Trade Quality Scanner

A mobile-friendly computer vision system that helps farmers prove the quality of their tomatoes and get fair prices.

---

## 🌾 Problem

Farmers often receive low prices because buyers judge produce quality subjectively.  
There is no quick, trusted way for farmers to prove the real quality of their harvest.

---

## ✅ Solution

Fair-Trade Quality Scanner lets farmers:
- Take a photo of their tomatoes
- Automatically analyze quality
- Get a clear Grade (A / B / C)
- View fair market price insights
- Download a digital quality certificate

This helps farmers negotiate with confidence.

---

## 📸 How It Works

1. Farmer opens the app  
2. Sees a **photo-taking guide** (how to take a good picture)  
3. Takes a photo of tomatoes on a white background  
4. Places a ₹5 or ₹10 coin for better size accuracy  
5. System analyzes the image  
6. Results and certificate are generated instantly
7. Based on quality grade assign, we are going to predict the prices of the commodity using past 7 to 14 days price history

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧭 Photo Guidance
- Simple visual instructions
- Shows what to do and what not to do
- Improves image quality before analysis

---

### 🪙 Coin-Based Size Reference (Optional)
- Farmer places a coin next to tomatoes
- Live camera shows a reference circle
- Improves size measurement confidence
- System works even without a coin

---

### 📊 Results Dashboard
- Tomato or any other commodity count
- Average size estimation
- Color consistency
- HSV color histogram (visual explanation)
- Measurement confidence indicator

---

### 🧾 Digital Quality Certificate
- Quality grade (A / B / C)
- Date and certificate ID
- QR code for verification
- Uploaded image included
- Downloadable as PDF

---

### 💰 Fair Price Insight
- Uses past 7–14 days market data
- Shows:
  - Minimum fair price
  - Average market price
  - Premium price for high quality

---

## 🔒 Trust & Reliability

- No hidden scoring
- No forced calibration
- No manual grading
- All new features are UI-only and safe
- Existing ML logic is never changed
